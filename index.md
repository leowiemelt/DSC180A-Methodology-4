1. **What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**
The most interesting topic was how consensus mechanisms like Proof of Work and Proof of Stake let parties who don’t trust each other agree on one record. It made me see blockchain as a general tool for verifiable data, not just cryptocurrency.
2. **Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**
For Quarter 2, I’d like to look at verifiable credentials, which are digitally signed, tamper-proof claims like degrees or licenses that can be checked without contacting the issuer. I want to see how a blockchain registry could handle issuing and revoking them while protecting the holder’s privacy.
3. **What is a potential change you’d make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**
I’d define clear evaluation criteria earlier, like verification speed and cost so we can measure whether the system actually works.
4. **What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**
I’d like to apply causal inference methods like difference-in-differences to on-chain data, such as measuring how protocol upgrades changed user behavior. I’m also interested in graph analysis of transaction networks.
